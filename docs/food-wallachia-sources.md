# Gastronomia: Valacchia

Third part of the Gastronomia chapter, shown for Valacchia: five food cards,
five traces with `where: 'region'`. Ciorbă de burtă is also a variant on the
whole-country Ciorbă e borș card (same trace, D6).

## Evidence consulted

- Mici: [Euronews România](https://www.euronews.ro/articole/mica-istorie-a-marelui-mic),
  [Newsweek România](https://newsweek.ro/timp-liber/unde-au-fost-inventati-mititeii-romanesti-cine-i-a-botezat-asa-reteta-de-mici-de-la-1872),
  [Bucharest.ro](https://www.bucharest.ro/articles/on-covaci-street-in-bucharest-romanian-mici-were-invented-and-caragiale-s-craii-de-curte-veche-used-to-celebrate-382)
  and [Jurnalul](https://jurnalul.ro/stiri/observator/istoria-micilor-mituri-urbane-si-traditii-843844.html)
  for the legend of the Covaci Street inn, the earlier casing-less recipes, the
  Balkan and Ottoman parallels, Orășanu's 1872 all-beef recipe (via Bacalbașa)
  and pork added later; [Playtech](https://playtech.ro/2026/de-ce-mancam-mici-de-1-mai-cat-costa-in-2026-acest-preparat-traditional-in-cele-mai-cunoscute-locuri-din-bucuresti/)
  for 1 May and Obor. The card presents the legend as a legend (B3).
- Ciorbă de burtă: [Observator](https://observatornews.ro/eveniment/ciorba-de-burta-motiv-de-cearta-intre-turci-si-greci-romanii-au-desavarsit-reteta-nam-vazuto-la-ei-652256.html),
  [Newsweek România](https://newsweek.ro/istorie/cine-a-inventat-ciorba-de-burta-legatura-lui-suleyman-magnificul-cu-felul-de-mancare-adorat-de-romani)
  and [BZI](https://www.bzi.ro/ciorba-de-burta-reteta-traditionala-romaneasca-4836318)
  for beef tripe, yolks and smântână, vinegar or lemon, garlic and chilli at the
  table, the Turkish işkembe çorbası and the dispute between countries. Dating
  (17th or 19th century) differs: left out. "The Romanians perfected it" is left
  out (B7).
- Covrigi: [Unknown Bucharest](https://unknownbucharest.com/romanian-traditional-food/the-famous-buzau-pretzels-covrigi-de-buzau/),
  [EVZ](https://evz.ro/covrigii-buzau-istoria.html) and
  [Puterea](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=257022) for the simigerii, Buzău as the
  covrigi town, a tradition of over 200 years, the Greek-merchant legend and the
  uncertain origin, the trademark after 1990.
- Magiun: [Avocatnet](https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_24013/Magiunul-de-prune-Topoloveni-primul-produs-romanesc-in-topul-calitatii-europene.html),

  and [BZI](https://www.bzi.ro/magiunul-de-prune-topoloveni-produs-romanesc-fara-zahar-adaugat-recunoscut-drept-cel-mai-bun-produs-din-fructe-din-lume-5461162)
  for the PGI of April 2011, the first Romanian food product with it, no added
  sugar, slow cooking and the 1914 recipe. "Best fruit product in the world"
  (TasteAtlas) is left out (B7).
- Dealu Mare: [Dealu Mare wine region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dealu_Mare_wine_region),
  [Regent Holidays](https://www.regent-holidays.co.uk/blog/romania-wine-regions-a-journey-through-dobrogea-dealu-mare-dragasani/)
  and [The Quirky Cork](https://thequirkycork.com/?p=20131) for the 65 km strip,
  3–12 km wide, between the Teleajen and the Buzău, Valea Călugărească (reds) and
  Pietroasele (whites), the PDO/DOC and Fetească neagră.

# Alba Iulia city card

The card opens the Transylvania part of the Cities chapter (alphabetical, before
Brașov). Alba Iulia joins the trip filter under Transilvania. Three places are
appended to the album; no existing ID or number changes. The 1918 event of the
timeline links to the Union Hall with `<Today>`, gated on Alba Iulia so the link
never points to a hidden card.

## Evidence consulted

- Citadel: the [Alba Iulia Fortress article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alba_Iulia_Fortress),
  the [county heritage record](https://www.apulum.ro/index.php/patrimoniu_en/cetatea_alba_carolina),
  [RRI](https://rri.ro/en/features-and-reports/rri-encyclopaedia/alba-carolina-fortress-id168235.html)
  and [Alba24](https://alba24.ro/foto-detalii-mai-putin-cunoscute-despre-cetatea-alba-carolina-300-de-ani-de-la-inceputul-lucrarilor-la-fortificatia-bastionara-de-la-alba-iulia-450441.html)
  for the Vauban-type seven-bastion star fort, Visconti's plan, the start of works in
  1715 under Charles VI, and its position over Apulum and the medieval citadel.
  Sources give 1714/1715 to 1738/1739: the card gives only the start, 1715.
- St Michael's Cathedral: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Michael%27s_Cathedral,_Alba_Iulia),
  the [county heritage record](https://apulum.ro/index.php/patrimoniu_en/biserica/569)
  (13th–16th century) and [Alba24](https://alba24.ro/11-august-1456-ziua-in-care-voievodul-iancu-de-hunedoara-a-decedat-este-inmormantat-in-catedrala-romano-catolica-din-alba-iulia-1094361.html)
  for Hunyadi's tomb in the south aisle and the joint Hungarian and Romanian
  commemorations. One article says the tomb was plundered in the Middle Ages, so the
  card calls it the tomb "attributed" to Hunyadi.
- Union Hall: [Agerpres](https://agerpres.ro/2023/11/28/reportaj-alba-sala-unirii-mai-mare-in-1918-decat-acum-neincapatoare-pentru-cei-1-228-de-delegati--1211263),
  [Alba24](https://alba24.ro/sala-unirii-simbol-al-marii-uniri-de-la-1-decembrie-1918-semnificatii-si-istoric-de-ce-nu-au-fost-facute-fotografii-1109394.html)
  and [Profit.ro](https://profit.ro/stiri/social/foto-arhiva-detalii-legate-de-marea-unire-de-la-alba-iulia-unele-cunoscute-doar-de-istorici-17422074)
  for the former military casino, the 1,228 delegates, and the 1921–1922 changes for
  the coronation. [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/alba-iulia/alba-iulia-catedrala-incoronarii-regilor-958951.html)
  and [Romania Insider](https://romania-insider.com/dec-1-celebration-history-nov-2020)
  for the Coronation Cathedral (1921–1922) and the coronation of 15 October 1922.
  The hall's construction year (1898–1901 or 1900) is omitted.

No opening hours, ticket prices or interior access are introduced.

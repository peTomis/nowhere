# Apuseni Mountains landscape card

Fourth card of the Natura chapter, shown for Transilvania. The Bihor side of
the range is Crișana, which the trip filter has no entry for: its two traces sit
with Transilvania, and their `place` says Crișana (as Timișoara's card says
Banat). Four places are appended to the album: the Scărișoara ice cave and the
Avram Iancu memorial house (Alba), the Bears' Cave and Cetățile Ponorului
(Bihor).

## Evidence consulted

- The range and the park: [Apuseni Natural Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apuseni_Natural_Park)
  for the park (law 5/2000; the infobox also gives 1990), 75,784 ha in Cluj,
  Bihor and Alba, the karst, the Padiș plateau and Cetățile Ponorului (14.9 ha);
  [Țara Moților](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C8%9Aara_Mo%C8%9Bilor) for the
  scattered Moți villages up to about 1,400 m, logging, woodworking and mining,
  Roșia Montană, and the 1784 and 1848 revolts starting in the region. "Highest
  permanent settlements of Romania" is left out (B7). The timeline already tells
  the Horea revolt.
- Scărișoara: [Scărișoara Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sc%C4%83ri%C8%99oara_Cave)
  for Gârda de Sus, the shaft of about 60 × 50 m with metal stairs, about
  75,000 m³ of ice up to about 20 m thick, about 0 °C in the show part, Racoviță's
  research; [Alba24](https://alba24.ro/ritmul-de-topire-a-ghetarului-de-la-scarisoara-cel-mai-accelerat-din-ultima-suta-de-ani-specialisti-internationali-in-apuseni-1118524.html)
  and [G4Media](https://www.g4media.ro/cercetare-internationala-in-pestera-ghetarul-de-la-scarisoara-pentru-reconstituirea-climatului-din-trecut-model-3d-obtinut-prin-scanarea-completa-a-pesterii.html)
  for the climate research and the faster melting; [Munții Noștri](https://muntii-nostri.ro/trails/download-atlas/sat-arieseni-pestera-ghetarul-scarisoara)
  for the marked path from Arieșeni. The 75,000 m³ figure dates from 1969 and no
  newer one was found; the age of the ice is still being studied: not given.
- Bears' Cave: [Bears' Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bears%27_Cave),
  [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/oradea/lucruri-nestiute-despre-pestera-ursilor-minunatia-1602357.html)
  and [Newsweek România](https://newsweek.ro/actualitate/misterul-pesterii-ursilor-din-muntii-apuseni-casa-zeilor-sau-acunzisul-a-140-de-ursi)
  for the blast in the Chișcău marble quarry on 17 September 1975, the miner
  Traian Curta, the opening on 14 July 1980, the cave bear remains and the
  constant 10 °C. The "140 skeletons" is left out: sources tie it to a legend, and
  others count far fewer complete skeletons.
- Cetățile Ponorului: [Newsweek România](https://newsweek.ro/actualitate/minunea-naturala-a-everestului-speologiei-romane-e-cel-mai-mare-complex-carstic-din-romania)
  and [Welcome to Romania](https://www.welcometoromania.eu/Apuseni/Apuseni_Padis_Cetatile_Ponorului_e.htm)
  for the three rock cirques in a forested depression about 300 m deep and over
  1 km wide and the portal; "largest karst complex of Romania" is left out (B7).
- Avram Iancu house: [cimec, Ghidul muzeelor](https://ghidulmuzeelor.cimec.ro/idEN.asp?k=51)
  and [Alba24](https://alba24.ro/astazi-complexul-muzeal-avram-iancu-inaugurat-oficial-a-fost-reabilitat-cu-fonduri-de-48-milioane-lei-de-la-ministerul-culturii-228471.html)
  for Incești (commune of Avram Iancu, formerly Vidra de Sus), the house built
  around 1800 by his parents, the birth in 1824, the museum opened by ASTRA in
  1924, the collections and the reopening in 2013; [Alba24](https://alba24.ro/testamentul-olograf-al-lui-avram-iancu-expus-in-localitatea-natala-cu-ocazia-targului-de-fete-de-pe-muntele-gaina-506497.html)
  for the fair on Mount Găina.

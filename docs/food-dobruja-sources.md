# Gastronomia: Dobrogea

Fifth and last part of the Gastronomia chapter, shown for Dobrogea: five food
cards, five traces with `where: 'region'`. Borș lipovenesc is also a variant on
the whole-country Ciorbă e borș card (same trace, D6).

## Evidence consulted

- Borș lipovenesc: [Euronews România](https://www.euronews.ro/articole/festivalul-borsului-de-peste-din-tulcea-peste-40-de-mii-de-portii-de-mancare-au-f)
  and [G4Media](https://www.g4media.ro/galerie-foto-se-pregatesc-50-000-de-portii-la-festivalul-borsului-de-peste-din-jurilovca-anunta-organizatorii.html)
  for the Jurilovca festival (40,000 portions served, 50,000 planned: the card
  says "decine di migliaia"), big pots over a wood fire, at least four Danube
  fish; [Jurnalul](https://jurnalul.ro/timp-liber/culinar/bucate-savuroase-ca-la-lipoveni-551758.html)
  (Radu Anton Roman) for few vegetables; [Spotmedia / ProTV](https://spotmedia.ro/stiri/timp-liber/secretul-ciorbei-de-peste-aflat-de-la-un-pescar-din-delta-dunarii-reportaj-la-sulina)
  for fishermen using only salt and vinegar although they call it borș.
- Saramură: [EVZ](https://evz.ro/reteta-saramurii-lipovenesti-care-a-intrat-in-gura-intregii-lumi.html)
  for fish over a live fire, coarse salt and plenty of chilli. Its UNESCO claim
  is left out (one source only). Mămăligă as the side: to confirm.
- Plăcintă dobrogeană: [România TV](https://www.romaniatv.net/placinta-dobrogeana-reteta-pastrata-si-neschimbata-de-generatii-intregi-care-e-povestea-ei_8505343.html),
  [Chef's Pencil](https://www.chefspencil.com/romanian-traditional-pie-placinta-dobrogeana/)
  and [Euronews România](https://www.euronews.ro/articole/placinta-dobrogreana-reteta-cu-traditie-de-ce-au-contestat-bulgarii-dreptul-roman)
  for hand-stretched sheets, salty cheese (telemea) and eggs, meat or sweet
  versions, the recipe handed down and Bulgaria contesting the Romanian
  registration.
- Șuberek: [Jurnalul](https://jurnalul.ro/timp-liber/culinar/suberekul-dobrogean-de-la-bucataria-otomana-pe-litoralul-romanesc-al-marii-negre-iata-reteta-915219.html),
  [Jurnalul, recipe](https://jurnalul.ro/timp-liber/culinar/cum-se-prepara-suberekul-deliciul-culinar-tataresc-988494.html)
  and [Mediafax](https://www.mediafax.ro/social/se-implinesc-142-de-ani-de-la-unirea-dobrogei-cu-romania-elise-ali-transmite-traditia-tatareasca-dobrogenilor-cu-ajutorul-mancarurilor-specifice-19744698/amp)
  for the crisp fried half-moon, meat and onion or cheese, the Tatar dish served
  to guests, the Ottoman cuisine.
- Murfatlar: [Financiarul](https://financiarul.ro/actualitate/podgoria-murfatlar/),
  [the eAmbrosia file](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/11250)
  and [Wein-Plus](https://glossary.wein-plus.eu/murfatlar) for the Dobrogea
  plateau between the Danube and the Black Sea, limestone soils, scarce water,
  experimental plantings from 1907 (one source: the card says "dall’inizio del
  Novecento"), sweet wines from overripe grapes, Chardonnay and Pinot gris;
  [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/constanta/constantaturistii-invitati-la-degustari-de-vin-858598.html)
  for tastings.

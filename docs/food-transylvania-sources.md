# Gastronomia: Transilvania

Second part of the Gastronomia chapter, shown for Transilvania, with the Banat,
Crișana and Maramureș (no entry of their own in the trip filter, as for the
cities). Seven food cards and nine traces, all `where: 'region'`. Pălincă and
horincă are also listed by the whole-country Țuică card, balmoș by the Mămăligă
card: the same traces (D6).

## Evidence consulted

- Salam de Sibiu: [Capital](https://www.capital.ro/romania-a-reusit-sa-obtina-protectia-si-recunoasterea-europeana-pentru-salamul-de-sibiu.html),
  [Financiarul](https://www.financiarul.ro/2016/02/19/romania-a-reusit-sa-obtina-protectia-si-recunoasterea-europeana-pentru-salamul-de-sibiu/)
  and [Startupcafe](https://www.startupcafe.ro/stiri-afaceri-20808695-salamul-sibiu-recunoscut-oficial-produs-traditional-romanesc-uniunea-europeana.htm)
  for the EU PGI of February 2016, the noble mould, the start of production
  around 1910 by Italians (Filippo Dozzi) in the Sibiu area (Mediaș) and the
  Prahova valley. Dates (1885, 1895, 1910) and place (Sinaia) differ: the card
  says "all’inizio del Novecento" and that the sources disagree.
- Kürtőskalács: [Kürtőskalács](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%BCrt%C5%91skal%C3%A1cs),
  [Hungarian Conservative](https://hungarianconservative.com/articles/culture_society/the-history-of-kurtoskalacs-trdelnik-prague-budapest)
  and [Acta Universitatis Sapientiae, "Whose is kürtőskalács?"](https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/ausp-2018-0028)
  for the spit over embers, the caramelised sugar, the Székely link, the
  Transylvanian cookbook of 1784, Baumstriezel and the disputed name.
- Varză à la Cluj: [Transtelex](https://telex.hu/transtelex/2025/11/09/kolozsvari-kaposzta-gasztronomia-tortenelem)
  and [Telex](https://telex.hu/gasztro/2021/12/22/hanyfele-toltott-kaposzta-letezik-a-vilagon-es-kie-az-igazi)
  for the 1695 "kolozsvári káposzta" (cabbage with goose or chicken), the later
  layered dish and the anecdotes about its invention; [Zserbo](https://zserbo.com/meat-dishes/kolozsvari-layered-sauerkraut/)
  for today's rice, pork, sausage and sour cream. No link to Gundel.
- Pălincă: [the EU technical file (DFAT)](https://www.dfat.gov.au/sites/default/files/200s.docx)
  for the GI, 40–52% vol., thirteen counties of the Carpathian arc including
  Bihor and Maramureș; [Newsweek România](https://newsweek.ro/timp-liber/unde-s-a-inventat-palinca-cat-de-romaneasca-este-pot-ungurii-sa-o-revendice)
  for ethnologist Ioan Godea placing the word in the Sătmar–Sălaj–Bihor area;
  [Digi24](https://www.digi24.ro/regional/digi24-oradea/un-singur-producator-traditional-a-fost-atestat-in-bihor-292351)
  and [BZI](https://www.bzi.ro/cum-sa-faci-cea-mai-buna-palinca-sau-tuica-de-gutui-secretul-din-bihor-al-celei-mai-bune-bauturi-distilate-5050469)
  for Bihor's stills (about 400), the fruits, the double distillation to about
  50° and the craft handed down in families; [Wine Wit and Wisdom](https://winewitandwisdomswe.com/?p=21354)
  for pălincă and pálinka as distinct under EU law.
- Banat: [Zile și Nopți](https://zilesinopti.ro/2026/04/17/regiune-gastronomica-europeana-banat/)
  for the European Region of Gastronomy 2028 and a cuisine shared by the
  communities for over three centuries; [Știrile ProTV](https://stirileprotv.ro/divers/gusturile-romaniei-intre-traditie-si-influente-europene.html)
  for noodles, potato soup and ham dishes with little spice (Swabian, Hungarian,
  German), papricaș and noodles with walnuts and sugar; [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/timisoara/secretele-plescavitei-deliciosul-hamburger-1730008.html)
  for the pljeskavica spreading in Timișoara after 1989.
- Horincă: [Țuică](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C8%9Auic%C4%83) for horincă
  and turț as names of țuică in Maramureș and Oaș and the twice-distilled
  strongest part.
- Balmoș: [Mămăligă](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%83m%C4%83lig%C4%83),
  [Culture Cheese Magazine](https://culturecheesemag.com/travel/cheesy-must-eats-in-romanias-carpathian-mountains/)
  and [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/baia-mare/cea-mai-buna-mancare-din-bucataria-1762384.html)
  for the shepherds' dish of the stână: fresh sheep cheese boiled in sheep's
  milk or cream, thickened with cornmeal.

# Piatra Craiului landscape card

Third card of the Natura chapter, shown for Transilvania. Four places on the
Brașov side of the ridge are appended to the album: the Zărnești gorges, the
Libearty bear sanctuary and the village of Măgura (all `nearby`, about 30 km
from Brașov) and the La Om peak (`remote`).

## Evidence consulted

- The park: [Piatra Craiului National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piatra_Craiului_National_Park),
  [the park regulation (mmediu.ro)](https://mmediu.ro/app/webroot/uploads/files/2016-03-23_REGULAMENT_Piatra_Craiului.pdf)
  and [the Council of Europe application for the European Diploma](https://rm.coe.int/piatra-craiului-national-park-romania-application-for-the-european-dip/168092a8f9)
  for the protection from 1938, the national park of 1990 (order 7/1990,
  confirmed by law 5/2000), the 25 km ridge, La Om (Piscul Baciului) at 2,238 m,
  the Jurassic limestone, the two counties (Brașov and Argeș), bears, wolves and
  lynx, and _Dianthus callizonus_, endemic, the park's symbol, flowering in the
  second half of July. Wikipedia gives Țimbalul Mare (2,177 m) as the highest
  peak: wrong, the park's own documents give La Om.
- Moieciu, Măgura and Peștera: [Moieciu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moieciu)
  for the commune in the Rucăr–Bran pass, about 29 km from Brașov, Măgura and
  Peștera on the eastern side of the ridge and the altitudes (794–1,200 m);
  [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/life/romania-photo-day-dreamstime-magura-village)
  and [Munții Noștri](https://www.muntii-nostri.ro/trails/download-atlas/ocolul-pietrei-craiului)
  for the Măgura hill as a viewpoint, the path to Peștera and the guesthouses.
- Zărnești gorges: [Munții Noștri](https://muntii-nostri.ro/trails/download-atlas/casa-folea-prapastiile-zarnestilor-2/)
  and [Dangerous Roads](https://dangerousroads.org/eastern-europe/romania/10743-travelling-through-the-defiant-gorges-of-zarnesti.html)
  for a length of 4–5 km (the card says "circa 4 km"), walls of 100–200 m, 5–6 m
  apart in places, the nature reserve and the climbers.
- Libearty: [Millions of Friends, visit the sanctuary](https://www.millionsoffriends.org/en/libearty/visit-the-sanctuary),
  [Zarnesti.net](https://zarnesti.net/en/useful-information/bear-sanctuary/)
  and [Visit Zărnești](https://www.visitzarnesti.ro/en/libearty-bear-sanctuary)
  for the foundation in 2005, the opening to visitors in 2008, about 69 ha, over
  100 bears from cages, circuses and private owners, guided visits only, Tuesday
  to Sunday. "Largest brown bear sanctuary in the world" is left out (B7).

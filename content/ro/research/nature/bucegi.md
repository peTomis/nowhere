# Bucegi landscape card

The ninth card of the Natura chapter, added on 2026-11-14. Two new traces
(`remote`, Wallachia): the Sphinx and Babele, and the Heroes' Cross on Caraiman.
The card also lists Peleș and the Cantacuzino castle, already traces of the Carol
I and Brașov cards.

## Evidence consulted

- [Bucegi Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucegi_Mountains): Omu
  2,505 m, Babele and Sphinx shaped by wind and rain on the plateau, the Bușteni
  cable car (Silva hotel – Babele chalet), Sinaia and Bușteni, the natural park by
  order 7/1990, Kogaionon and Zalmoxis.
- [Heroes' Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes%27_Cross_on_Caraiman_Peak):
  1926–1928, Queen Marie and King Ferdinand, railway workers who died in World
  War I, 28 m (39.3 m with the pedestal), 2,291 m, lit at night, Guinness 2014.
- [Antena 3](https://www.antena3.ro/actualitate/tarife-telecabina-busteni-calatorie-babele-2024-698038.html)
  for the cable car (prices and hours change: the text gives neither); the upper
  station is about 230 m from Babele and 500 m from the Sphinx (travel guides).
- [Munții Noștri](https://www.muntii-nostri.ro/trails/download-atlas/busteni-cabana-padina-pietrosita):
  Bușteni – Jepii Mici – Caraiman, 3–4 h, difficult, closed in winter.
- Coordinates: Sphinx from Wikidata Q3623938 (OpenStreetMap places Babele at the
  same spot; Wikidata's Babele item is a few km off); the cross from
  OpenStreetMap.
- Photos: Stefan Jurca (CC BY-SA 2.0), Dragoș Asaftei (CC BY-SA 3.0 ro), Commons.

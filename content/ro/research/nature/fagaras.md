# Făgăraș Mountains landscape card

Second card of the Natura chapter, shown for Transilvania and Valacchia (the
ridge was the border between them). Three places are appended to the album: the
Vidraru dam and the Moldoveanu peak (Argeș county, Valacchia) and Bâlea Lake
(Sibiu county, Transilvania). The card opens with Poenari, already a trace of
Vlad III: the same place, with a landscape text in `traceTexts` (D6).

## Evidence consulted

- The range: [Făgăraș Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C4%83g%C4%83ra%C8%99_Mountains)
  for Moldoveanu (2,544 m), the eight peaks above 2,500 m (Negoiu among them),
  the Olt valley to the west, the Făgăraș depression to the north and the
  glacial lakes (Bâlea the largest, Podragu the deepest); [Fanatik](https://www.fanatik.ro/top-5-cele-mai-spectaculoase-lacuri-glaciare-din-romania-locurile-speciale-care-i-lasa-pe-turisti-fara-cuvinte-21005648)
  for Moldoveanu as the highest peak of Romania at 2,544 m.
- Transfăgărășan: [Transfăgărășan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transf%C4%83g%C4%83r%C4%83%C8%99an),
  [RFE/RL](https://rferl.org/amp/romania-transfagarasan-road-50th-anniversary/33125063.html),
  [Economica.net](https://www.economica.net/49-de-ani-de-transfagarasan-constructia-celui-mai-spectaculos-drum-din-romania-urgentata-de-invadarea-cehoslovaciei_694691.html)
  and [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stil-de-viata/magazin/cum-au-fost-construite-marile-sosele-din-carpati-2396753.html)
  for the works by army engineer troops from 10 March 1970, the opening on 20
  September 1974, the link to the 1968 invasion of Czechoslovakia, the 40
  official deaths and the "hundreds" of the testimonies, the 884 m Bâlea tunnel
  and the closure from late October to late June. The length (90 or 151 km) is
  left out: sources measure different stretches.
- Vidraru: [Vidraru Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidraru_Dam),
  [Structurae](https://structurae.net/structures/vidraru-dam) and
  [Agerpres](https://agerpres.ro/economic/2024/07/09/arges-a-fost-semnat-contractul-pentru-retehnologizarea-hidrocentralei-vidraru-investitie-de-peste-20--1324906)
  for the 166 m arch dam, the 305 m crest (bzi.ro: 307 m; the card says "circa
  300 m"), completion in 1966 and the plant in service from 9 December 1966.
  "Fifth in Europe" at its opening is left out (B7).
- Bâlea Lake: [Sibiu City App](https://www.sibiucityapp.ro/en/places/caldarea-glaciara-balea-8c3rqgomkyqfga?page=9),
  [Munții Noștri](https://muntii-nostri.ro/trails/download-atlas/cabana-balea-cascada-valea-balea-lacul-balea/)
  and [GeoEcoMar](https://geoecomar.ro/website/publicatii/supliment2009/7.pdf)
  for 2,034 m (one study gives 2,040 m, Wikipedia 2,100 m), 4.65 ha, a maximum
  depth of about 11–12 m, the largest glacial lake of the range and the Bâlea
  nature reserve.

# Retezat landscape card

Sixth card of the Natura chapter, shown for Transilvania (Hunedoara county).
Three places are appended to the album, all `remote` and reached only on foot:
Bucura and Zănoaga lakes and the Peleaga peak.

## Evidence consulted

- The park: [Retezat National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retezat_National_Park)
  for 1935 (Tătărescu government, 100 km²), the first national park of Romania,
  the UNESCO MAB reserve of 1979, about 80 glacial lakes (54 permanent), Peleaga
  at 2,509 m, more than sixty peaks above 2,300 m, the Gemenele scientific
  reserve (about 1,800 ha of old-growth forest), wolves, bears and lynx;
  [Puterea](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=58734) and [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/hunedoara/excursie-in-cel-mai-vechi-parc-din-tara-1157957.html)
  for the first park, the Council of Ministers decision of 22 March 1935 and the
  initiative of Alexandru Borza (with Emil Racoviță from 1923). Permanent lakes:
  54 or 58 by source; the card says "più di cinquanta".
- Bucura: [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/blogurile-adevarul/bucuraa-te-de-retezat-cum-ajungi-in-doua-ore-la-1541355.html),
  [Financiarul](https://financiarul.ro/actualitate/lacul-bucura-cel-mai-mare-lac-glaciar-din-romania/),
  [SummitPost](https://Summitpost.org/alpine-lakes-of-retezat-mountains/658515)
  and [List of lakes of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_lakes_of_Romania)
  for the largest glacial lake of Romania, about 2,040 m, 8.8–10.5 ha by source
  (the card gives the range), two hours on foot by the shortest route;
  [Pixeo](https://pixeoapp.com/photography-guides/europe/romania/lacul-bucura)
  for the start from Cârnic.
- Zănoaga: [BZI](https://www.bzi.ro/care-sunt-cele-mai-adanci-lacuri-din-romania-foto-1177645)
  and [SummitPost](https://Summitpost.org/alpine-lakes-of-retezat-mountains/658515)
  for about 29 m, the deepest glacial lake of Romania (Fanatik gives 26 m), at
  1,997–2,030 m by source and 6.5 ha.

## Verified (2026-11-13)

- Poiana Pelegii: asphalt to Gura Apelor, then about 20 km of forest road;
  about two hours to Bucura ([Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/blogurile-adevarul/bucuraa-te-de-retezat-cum-ajungi-in-doua-ore-la-1541355.html)).
- Camping only in designated zones (Poiana Pelegii, Bucura, Zănoaga and
  others): [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/romania/retezat-mountains/attractions/retezat-national-park/a/poi-sig/1289916/1326161),
  [GO Hunedoara](https://www.gohunedoara.com/?p=20109). The text doesn't name
  Bucura–Zănoaga; Bucura–Peleaga "un paio d'ore" fits the trail sheets.

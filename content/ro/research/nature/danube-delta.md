# Danube Delta landscape card

The first card of the Natura chapter (chapter 5), and the first landscape card
of the guide. Four places are appended to the album, all in Dobrogea and all
`remote`: the old lighthouse of Sulina, the Letea forest, the harbour of
Jurilovca and the Enisala fortress (the last two on the Razim–Sinoe lagoons,
which are part of the biosphere reserve).

## Evidence consulted

- The delta: [Danube Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danube_Delta) for the
  three arms (Chilia carries the most water, about 3,800 m³/s against 1,500 and
  1,250), the Romanian share (3,446 km², about 83%), the Razim–Sinoe lagoons
  "geologically and ecologically related" to the delta, the corrections of the
  Sulina arm from 1862 (92 to 64 km), the Lipovans and the large-scale drainage
  after 1960. [Radio Constanța](https://radioconstanta.ro/2021/09/01/1-septembrie-ziua-rezervatiei-biosferei-delta-dunarii-2)
  for HG 983/1990 creating the biosphere reserve and its administration
  (ARBDD), the UNESCO MAB network in 1990 and the World Heritage List in 1991.
  [Gândul](https://www.gandul.ro/magazin/planul-diabolic-al-lui-ceausescu-de-a-distruge-delta-dunarii-dictatorul-a-incercat-sa-faca-din-bijuteria-naturala-unesco-al-doilea-baragan-20942643)
  for the "Programul de amenajare şi exploatare integrală a Deltei Dunării" of
  28 March 1983; [Newsweek România](https://newsweek.ro/actualitate/cum-a-macelarit-romania-delta-dunarii-inainte-sa-apara-pericolul-bistroe-1753-km-de-canale-noi)
  and [Digi24 on Carasuhat](https://www.digi24.ro/regional/digi24-constanta/delta-castiga-1-000-de-hectare-zona-carasuhat-mahmudia-este-renaturata-432238)
  for the embanked areas and their partial return to water after 1990. No areas
  or percentages are given in the text: the figures differ between sources
  (Wikipedia, Newsweek) and by the date of the works.
- Sulina lighthouse: [cimec, Ghidul muzeelor](https://ghidulmuzeelor.cimec.ro/idEN.asp?k=1536)
  for the construction by the European Danube Commission in 1869–1870, Charles
  Hartley, the museum run by ICEM Tulcea, the rooms on the Commission and Jean
  Bart, the address (Strada II nr. 15) and access by water only;
  [Arhitectura 1906, lighthouse](https://arhitectura-1906.ro/en/2022/08/lighthouse-at-the-end-of-the-danube/)
  for the older tower (probably Russian, around 1839) repaired by the
  Commission, the plaque "completed in November 1870" and the distance of about
  2.5 km from the shore; [Arhitectura 1906, Sulina](https://arhitectura-1906.ro/en/2022/08/legends-of-the-sulina/)
  for the works from 1857 to 1902 under Hartley and the arm shortened by more
  than 20 km. Restoration from 2019 ([Radio Constanța](https://radioconstanta.ro/2022/11/12/tulcea-lucrarile-de-reabilitare-a-farului-vechi-din-sulina-nereceptionate-inca));
  open again in 2025 ([Agerpres](https://agerpres.ro/2025/05/03/reportaj-tulcea-festivalul-scrumbiei-albastre-a-deschis-sezonul-turistic-la-sulina--1445212)).
  The card says "rifatto su una torre più antica" and does not date the older
  tower (1802, 1839 and 1841 all appear in sources).
- Letea: [Letea Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letea_Forest) and
  [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stil-de-viata/video-colt-de-rai-padurea-letea-cel-mai-exotic-1296111.html)
  for the protection in 1938, the oak forest on the dunes, the lianas and
  _Periploca graeca_ at its northern limit; [Financiarul](https://financiarul.ro/actuale/pe-urmele-cailor-din-padurea-letea-rezervatia-deltei-dunarii/)
  and [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/project-to-protect-letea-forest-and-local-half-wild-horse-herd-in-the-danube-delta)
  for the feral horses and the dispute over their impact. "Oldest reserve of
  Romania" (Wikipedia) against "second after Retezat" (Adevărul): the card says
  only "protetto dal 1938". "Northernmost subtropical forest of Europe" is left
  out (B7). Access with an ARBDD agent on a marked route: tour operators and
  travel guides only.
- Jurilovca: [Agerpres 2023](https://agerpres.ro/2023/06/19/reportaj-tulcea-comunitatea-traditionala-de-pescari-din-jurilovca-se-schimba--1126341)
  and [Agerpres 2025](https://agerpres.ro/social/2025/04/14/tulcea-gura-portitei-se-deschide-oaspetilor-in-luna-iunie-galerie-foto--1440455)
  for the foundation by Lipovans at the start of the 19th century, the fishing
  centre at the end of the 19th, the decline at the end of the 20th, the new
  harbour of June 2023 on Lake Golovița and the transport to Gura Portiței.
  [ICR, The Lipovan Russians of Romania](https://icr.ro/pagini/the-lipovan-russians-of-romania)
  for Nikon's reforms (1652–1658), the persecution and Jurilovca among the early
  Lipovan villages. The timeline's Context "I lipoveni" already places their
  arrival between the late 17th and the 18th century.
- Enisala: [Peuce, n.s. IX (2011)](https://revistapeuce.icemtl.ro/wp-content/uploads/Arhiva-Peuce-Serie-noua/08-Peuce-SN-IX-2011/26-Baltres-Stanciu-sn.9.pdf)
  and [Citadina](https://citadina.substack.com/p/plebeulamuzeu-cetatea-enisala-singura-cetate-medievala-din-dobrogea)
  for the 14th-century walls, the competing attributions (Genoese merchants,
  Byzantines, a Tatar prince, Mircea the Elder), the gulf closed by sand bars
  into the Razim lagoon, the abandonment in the 16th century under the Ottomans
  and the view over lakes Razim and Babadag. The card names the debate and takes
  no side (B3).

## Verified (2026-11-13)

- Enisala is run by ICEM Tulcea ([ICEM](https://www.icemtl.ro/enisala-medieval-fortress/)),
  but the trace was dropped from the album (see `album/excluded-traces.md`);
  its two entries in `sources.ts` may no longer be needed.
- Letea: access only with an ARBDD agent, a small visit fee
  ([Economica 2019](https://www.economica.net/taxa-de-vizitare-in-padurea-letea-se-poate-plati-si-prin-sms-incepand-de-vineri_171294.html)).
- 1938: Council of Ministers decision no. 645; protected since 1930, a reserve
  from 1938 per the Ministry of the Environment
  ([HG 2014](https://mmediu.ro/uploads/files/2014-04-23_HG.pdf)).
- Stock photographs: done (Wikimedia Commons, with author and licence in `structure.ts`).

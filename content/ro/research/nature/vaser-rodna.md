# Vaser Valley and Rodna Mountains landscape card

Seventh card of the Natura chapter. Maramureș has no entry in the trip filter:
the card and its traces sit with Transilvania, and the traces' `place` says
Maramureș. Three places are appended to the album: the Mocănița forest railway
at Vișeu de Sus, the Horses' Waterfall above Borșa and the Pietrosul Rodnei
peak.

## Evidence consulted

- Mocănița: [mocanita.ro](https://www.mocanita.ro/about-mocanita/) for the line
  of 1933, 760 mm, about 45 km in daily service, trains going up empty and coming
  back with logs, the tourist route to Paltin (21 km); [Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/moc%C4%83ni%C5%A3a-last-of-the-carpathian-forestry-railways-mocanita/hwWBAhG2oQ0A8A?hl=en-GB),
  [RHN](https://rhn.ro/en/mocanita-of-viseu-de-sus-a-journey-along-the-vaser-valley/)
  and [Playtech](https://playtech.ro/2023/mocanita-din-viseu-unica-in-lume-de-ce-trebuie-sa-parcurgi-traseele-cu-trenurile-cu-abur-indragite-de-turistii-din-intreaga-lume/)
  for construction from 1932, the 1929 flood that burst the Macârlău dam and led
  to the railway, the damage during the army's retreat and the rebuilding in
  1946–1948, the tourist trains from 2005. 1932 or 1933: the card says "all’inizio
  degli anni Trenta". One source says log transport ended in 2000, the
  railway's site says it goes on: the card says the line was built for the logs
  and is still in service, and does not say it still carries them. "Last steam
  forest railway in Europe" is left out (B7).
- Rodna: [Rodna Mountains National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodna_Mountains_National_Park)
  for the park (1990, law 5/2000) in Bistrița-Năsăud, Maramureș and Suceava and
  the weather station near the summit; [Radio Romania International](https://rri.ro/en/features-and-reports/green-planet/the-rodna-mountains-national-park-id153268.html)
  and [International Parks](https://www.internationalparks.org/romania/Rodna)
  for the reserve of 1932 on Pietrosul, Pietrosul Rodnei at 2,303 m, the highest
  peak of the Eastern Carpathians, and lake Iezer in the cirque under it.
- Horses' Waterfall: [Spotmedia](https://spotmedia.ro/stiri/timp-liber/cascada-cailor-din-borsa-de-ce-sa-vizitezi-acest-loc-si-ce-alte-obiective-gasesti-in-zona-lmt),
  [Digi24](https://www.digi24.ro/magazin/timp-liber/vacante/legenda-cumplita-din-muntii-rodnei-758059)
  and [EVZ](https://evz.ro/cascada-cailor-romania-atractii-turistice.html) for
  about 1,300 m, the glacial cirque, the limestone step, a height of 80–97 m by
  source, the chairlift from Borșa Complex and about half an hour on foot, and
  the legend. "Highest waterfall of Romania" is left out (B7).
- The wooden churches of Maramureș on the UNESCO list (1999): general
  knowledge, to be given a source.

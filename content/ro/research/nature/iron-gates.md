# Iron Gates landscape card

Fifth card of the Natura chapter, shown for Valacchia (Mehedinți county, filed
there as the Decebalus sculpture already is). The card opens with the Decebalus
rock sculpture, already a trace of Decebalus: the same place, with a landscape
text in `traceTexts` (D6). Three places are appended to the album: the Mraconia
monastery, the Iron Gates I dam and the ruins of Trajan's bridge. The Tabula
Traiana is named only in the introduction (as "the road along the bank now
Serbian"): it is in Serbia.

The same commit changes "fra le province di Brașov e di Argeș" to "fra i
distretti" in the Piatra Craiului card: the guide calls the județe "distretti".

## Evidence consulted

- The gorges: [Iron Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Gates) for the
  134 km route, the Great Kazan narrowing to 150 m, the _limes moesicus_, the
  Iron Gate I dam begun in 1964 with Yugoslavia and opened in 1972, the water
  raised by 35 m at the dam, old Orșova and Ada Kaleh submerged with about
  17,000 people moved, Ada Kaleh passing to Romania by the Treaty of Lausanne
  (1923) and the Romanian natural park from 2001;
  [Tabula Traiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabula_Traiana) for the road
  along the Danube completed around AD 100, on the Serbian bank.
- Ada Kaleh: [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/ada-kaleh-iron-gates),
  [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stil-de-viata/cultura/48-de-ani-de-la-scufundarea-insulei-ada-kaleh-a-1874277.html)
  and [Newsweek România](https://newsweek.ro/actualitate/paradisul-de-pe-dunare-care-a-fost-inghitit-de-ape-cum-a-fost-sacrificata-ada-kaleh-de-comunisti)
  for the flooding in 1970 and the islanders moved to Constanța or Turkey.
- Mraconia: [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/turnu-severin/se-implinesc-20-de-ani-de-la-reconstructia-sfintei-1464659.html),
  [Pro-Mehedinți](https://promehedinti.ro/en/manastirea-mraconia-2/) and
  [EVZ](https://evz.ro/turism-in-romania-opt-lacasuri-de-cult-care-sunt-in-picioare-si-astazi-chiar-daca-au-fost-inghitite-de-ape-in-urma-cu-zeci-de-ani.html)
  for the old monastery demolished during the dam works (1967) and flooded, "the
  monastery under the waters", the new church from 1993 to 1995 and the royal
  doors in the parish museum at Eșelnița.
- Trajan's bridge: [Trajan's Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trajan%27s_Bridge),
  [Pro-Mehedinți](https://promehedinti.ro/en/podul-lui-traian/) and
  [Euronews România](https://www.euronews.ro/articole/muzeul-regiunii-de-fier-drobeta-turnu-severin-podul-traian-expozitie-istorie)
  for Apollodorus of Damascus, 103–105, about 1,135 m, the surviving piers at
  Drobeta-Turnu Severin and Kostol, and the museum's reconstruction.

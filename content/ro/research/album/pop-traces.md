# Romania: "pop" traces

On 2026-11-14 eight dishes and drinks left the album (`excluded-traces.md`, third round): food
photos were 21.7% of it, over the 15% that `docs/prompt-for-new-guide.md` §B13 now allows. Seven of
the places went to "pop" traces, the eighth to Corvin Castle (see below). "Pop" traces are places a
traveller goes to anyway, which show the country as it is lived today and not only its monuments.
§B13 admits a café, a restaurant or a shop only when it is an institution: open for years in the
same place, known also to those who do not go in, recognisable from outside. Venues and shops count
apart from buildings in the quota on the subject of the photos.

## Evidence consulted

- Caru’ cu Bere: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caru%27_cu_Bere) and the
  [restaurant’s history page](https://www.carucubere.ro/en/history/) for 1879 on Calea Victoriei,
  Ioan Căbășan from a Transylvanian village and his Mircea nephews, the plot bought by Nicolae
  Mircea in 1897, the 1899 building by Siegfried Kofczinski (spelt Kofsinsky by the restaurant),
  Gothic Revival outside. Wikipedia alone: nationalised in 1949, returned to the heirs in 1999; the
  restaurant lists decorations, paintings and stained glass among its sections.
- Cărturești Carusel: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C4%83rture%C8%99ti_Carusel) and
  [Bucharest.ro](https://www.bucharest.ro/articles/carturesti-carusel-the-jewel-inside-the-chrissoveloni-palace-the-history-of-the-most-beautiful-bookstore-in-romania-751)
  for the Chrissoveloni bank headquarters, the communist-era store (Bucharest.ro names it
  «Familia»: one source, left out), the decay, the restoration finished in 2015 and the white,
  top-lit interior. Left out, one source each: six floors, 10,000 books, the year the building was
  built (1860, 1903 or 1904 by source).
- Strada Sforii: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strada_Sforii) and
  [Explore Carpathia](https://explorecarpathia.eu/en/romania/brasso-brasov/rope-street) for the
  width (111–135 cm), the 17th-century mention (1674 in Wikipedia) and the firefighters’ passage,
  written as a tradition: no primary source was found. Length 80 m: Wikipedia.
- Bolta Rece: [Financiarul](https://financiarul.ro/actualitate/bolta-rece-de-peste-228-de-ani-academia-ieseana-de-umor-si-inteligenta/)
  and [Zile și Nopți](https://zilesinopti.ro/2020/10/15/5-restaurante-care-au-facut-istorie/) for
  the first mention on 17 January 1786, Junimea and «universitatea vinurilor», Eminescu and
  Creangă (written as a tradition), Strada Rece 10, still a restaurant. TODO immagine: no licensed
  photograph on Wikimedia Commons; an 1875 caricature of a Junimea banquet «la Borta Rece» is
  there, not used.
- Marginea: [Financiarul](https://financiarul.ro/actualitate/ceramica-neagra-de-marginea-suceava-cunoscuta-in-lume-pentru-tehnica-preistorica-de-ardere-inabusita/)
  and [Homo Faber](https://www.homofaber.com/en/artisans/corneliu_magop%C4%83%C8%9B-ceramics-marginea)
  for about three centuries of pottery, the smothered firing with wood in a closed kiln, polishing
  with a river stone (Homo Faber), the Magopăț workshop at Calea Bucovinei 192 and the few families
  left (Homo Faber: from about sixty to three).
- Vama: [the museum’s site](https://www.muzeuloului-vama.com/),
  [Euronews România](https://www.euronews.ro/articole/18-mii-de-oua-o-singura-pasiune-muzeul-oului-vama-bucovina-detine-o-colectie-de-o)
  and [cIMeC](https://ghidulmuzeelor.cimec.ro/idEN.asp?k=2015) for Letiția Orșivschi, the family
  eggs the museum started from, the Bucovina and world sections, the workshop and the address. The
  number of eggs varies too much by source (3,000 to 18,000) to be given. Not to be confused with
  the Lucia Condrea museum, in Moldovița.
- Mamaia: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamaia) and
  [Radio Romania International](https://www.rri.ro/en/features-and-reports/travellers-guide/start-of-the-summer-season-in-mamaia-resort-id149319.html)
  for the 8 km strip between the sea and Lake Siutghiol, the opening in 1906 with a railway from
  Constanța, the 1960s hotels for foreign tourists and the 2004 gondola lift (Wikipedia).

## Taken out: the Hotel Castel Dracula

It was added first, on the Vlad III card, as the Stoker side of the Dracula myth next to Bran. Taken
out the same day: the hotel was put up for sale in 2018 (Wikipedia) and a Komoot page calls it
empty, while booking sites still list it; nothing confirms it is open. A building on a remote pass
that may be closed fails the tests of stability and of being worth the stop (§B13). Its place went
to Corvin Castle, under the 1441–1456 event of the timeline
(`research/history/culture-and-additions.md`). Its texts are in `excluded-traces.md` (third round).

shape with a tower and an inner courtyard, the building years (1976–1983; Wikipedia says built in 1976) and the name taken after 1989; [Dracula](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/345/345-h/345-h.htm)
for the Count’s carriage waiting at the Borgo Pass. TODO verificare: the hotel was put up for sale

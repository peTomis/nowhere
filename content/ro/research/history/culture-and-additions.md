# Culture, sport and monuments added on 2026-11-14

Additions after the 1541–1818 timeline was cut by about a third, to cover what a
traveller sees and hears beyond politics.

## Evidence consulted

- Language (Context "Una lingua neolatina", Dacia and Rome):
  [Romanian alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_alphabet), Latin
  alphabet decreed for official publications in 1862.
- Biertan (trace, Sighișoara card):
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biertan_fortified_church): 1486–1524,
  three rings of walls, Lutheran bishops 1572–1867, the sacristy lock (13 points,
  shown in Paris in 1889), the "matrimonial prison", UNESCO 1993. Coordinates:
  Wikidata Q16743875. Photo: Florio1974 (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Horezu (trace under the Brâncoveanu event, in no card):
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horezu_Monastery) (founded 1690,
  nunnery, UNESCO 1993), [UNESCO ICH](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/craftsmanship-of-horezu-ceramics-00610)
  (ceramics, 2012). Coordinates: Wikidata Q733069. Photo: Cristian Bortes
  (CC BY 2.0).
- Dada (curiosity, 1916): [Tristan Tzara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tristan_Tzara),
  [Marcel Janco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Janco).
- "Tre romeni a Parigi": Eliade and Cioran's Iron Guard sympathies, Ionesco's
  [Rhinoceros](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhinoceros_(play)>) (1959), Eliade
  in Chicago.
- Cinema: [Romanian New Wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_New_Wave)
  (Puiu, Un Certain Regard 2005; Mungiu, Palme d'Or 2007; Jude, Golden Bear 2021).
- Halep: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simona_Halep) (No. 1 on
  9 October 2017, Roland Garros 2018, Wimbledon 2019, CAS reduction in 2024,
  retired 2025); Năstase first ATP No. 1 in 1973.
- Cluj festivals: [TIFF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transilvania_International_Film_Festival)
  (2002), [UNTOLD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untold_Festival) (2015, Cluj
  Arena), [Electric Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_Castle) (Bonțida).

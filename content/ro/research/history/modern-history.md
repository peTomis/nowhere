# Romania: modern-history extension

Editorial scope: continue after the existing 1818 endpoint. The four added parts are
1821–1881, 1907–1947, 1948–1989 and 1990–2025. The last dated milestone is the 2025
presidential election; 2026 is not assigned an invented event. Earlier entries and album
identifiers are unchanged. Dates remain in `structure.ts`, labels in `it/names.ts`.

## Sources consulted

These pages were opened during the edit. This ledger records the evidence used; it is
not a certification that every sentence has two independent sources.

- [Romania: A Country Study, Library of Congress](https://countrystudies.us/romania/):
  historical chapters on the Russian protectorate, 1848, unification, the monarchy,
  the world wars, communist institutions, demographic policy and the 1989–1990 transition.
  Particularly chapters 11–19, 21–22, 26–27, 30–31, 37 and the introductory 1989–1990 update.
- [Area Handbook for Romania, Keefe et al., 1972](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/32700/32700-h/32700-h.htm):
  historical overview and education chapter, used to cross-check the nineteenth-century
  sequence, Cuza's reforms, the monarchy and the communist takeover. Its national and
  Cold War interpretations are not adopted. Its incorrect 1826–1828 war date and 1858
  date for the peace ending the Crimean War are not reproduced.
- [Dennis Deletant, Romania, 1948–1989: A Historical Overview](https://phpisn.ethz.ch/lory1.ethz.ch/collections/coll_romania/introduction0445.html?navinfo=15342):
  political control, foreign-policy autonomy, urbanisation, austerity and reproductive coercion.
- [Sighet Memorial, Communist repression](https://turmemorialsighet.ro/3-represiunea-comunista/):
  Securitate, imprisonment and forced labour, including the canal.
- [RomArchive, The Roma Movement in Romania](https://www.romarchive.eu/rom/roma-civil-rights-movement/roma-movement-romania/):
  abolition in 1855 and 1856 and the lack of support for emancipated people.
  Its deportation totals are not used.
- [US Holocaust Memorial Museum, Romania](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/romania):
  the 1940 territorial losses, Antonescu, Iași, deportations and the separate administration
  of northern Transylvania. No disputed casualty total is introduced.
- [Museum With No Frontiers / National Museum of Romanian History](https://sharinghistory.museumwnf.org/database_item.php?id=object%3BAWE%3Brm%3B3%3Ben):
  the 1860 Cernavodă–Constanța railway under Ottoman administration.
- [NATO, accession on 29 March 2004](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2004/03/29/seven-new-members-join-nato).
- [European Union, Romania country profile](https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/eu-countries/romania_en):
  EU accession on 1 January 2007.
- [European Commission, Schengen accession](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/news/bulgaria-and-romania-join-schengen-area-2025-01-03_en):
  separate air/sea (2024) and land (2025) dates.
- [House of Commons Library, 2025 presidential election](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10355/):
  annulment in December 2024 and the May 2025 rerun. Alleged interference is not
  presented as an independently proven Russian operation.

## Remaining editorial checks

The draft covers the requested chronological extension, but the strict two-independent-source
rule in `prompt-for-new-guide.md` is not yet satisfied for every detail. In particular,
obtain a second independent source for the railway opening, local memorial descriptions,
abolition compensation, canal completion, the 1991 constitution and the contemporary
milestones. The general histories above share a US government publishing context and
should not be treated as independent by default where one repeats the other's account.
A claim-by-claim second-source review is still needed before calling this fully fact-checked.

Keith Hitchins, _A Concise History of Romania_ (2014), was located on Cambridge's site,
but the full chapters were not accessible; its catalogue and abstracts are not factual
corroboration. The four attempted Britannica history URLs could not be opened and are
not counted as evidence.

No new images, traces or image TODOs are introduced. Existing image-credit warnings and
pre-1818 chronology warnings are outside this edit. The source-check TODO next to the
1860 event is intentional.

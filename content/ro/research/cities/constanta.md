# Constanța city card

Constanța is the first city card of the Dobrogea region: the region gets its own
part of the Città chapter ("Dobrogea", after Transilvania). Four places are
appended to the album. The 1877–1878 event of the timeline links to two of them
(the Ovid statue and the mosque, both built by the Romanian state after 1878)
with `<Today>`, gated on Constanța.

## Evidence consulted

- City history: [Constanța](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constan%C8%9Ba) for the
  Greek foundation of Tomis (6th century BC; other pages say 7th: the card names
  neither the century's dispute nor Miletus), Ovid's exile in AD 8, Tomis as
  capital of Scythia Minor from Diocletian, the Ottoman name Köstence, the 1878
  passage to Romania and the 1896 census (Romanians, Greeks, Tatars, Bulgarians,
  Turks, Jews). The timeline's existing Context on Roman Dobruja and the 1860
  railway event already state the capital and the British railway.
  [Agerpres, 110 years of the port](https://agerpres.ro/documentare/2019/09/27/documentar-110-ani-de-la-inaugurarea-oficiala-a-portului-constanta--375928)
  for the 1857 concession to the Danube and Black Sea Railway and Kustendge
  Harbour Company, the works begun on 16 October 1896, Anghel Saligny as director
  from 1899, the inauguration on 27 September 1909 in the presence of Carol I and
  the port as the largest maritime port of Romania.
- Ovid statue: [curatorial.ro](https://curatorial.ro/en/art/monumental-art-ovidiu-the-singer-of-gentle-loves/),
  [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/points-of-interest/statue-of-ovid/1285086)
  and [Ettore Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ettore_Ferrari) for the
  bronze by Ettore Ferrari, 1887, commissioned after the War of Independence as a
  sign of the link between Dobrogea and Rome, its place in front of the former
  city hall (now the Museum of National History and Archaeology) and the replica
  in Sulmona in 1925. One page says Ferrari finished it in 1883: not used.
- Roman Mosaic Edifice: [Digi24](https://www.digi24.ro/trecutul-din-prezent/edificiul-roman-cu-mozaic-din-constanta-3211927)
  and [Observator](https://observatornews.ro/eveniment/un-important-monument-istoric-din-constanta-restaurat-de-o-companie-italiana-a-fost-descoperit-intamplator-in-1959-585071.html)
  for the chance discovery in 1959 during building works on the cliff, the 4th
  century, the three terraces and the polychrome mosaic; [EVZ](https://evz.ro/edificiul-roman-cu-mozaic-din-constanta-a-fost-redeschis-intrarea-este-gratuita-pana-la-sfarsitul-anului.html)
  for the reopening on 14 November 2025. The English Wikipedia page says the
  mosaic was found during the 1860 railway works: wrong, every other source gives 1959. Digi24 calls it the city's "economic forum" and gives about 2,000 m² of
  mosaic; Wikipedia calls it a port building: the card says "probabilmente un
  luogo di commerci legato al porto" and gives no area. "Largest Roman-Byzantine
  mosaic in the Balkans" is left out (B7).
- Grand Mosque: [Grand Mosque of Constanța](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Mosque_of_Constan%C8%9Ba)
  and [InfoCons, after the Institutul Național al Patrimoniului](https://infocons.ro/infocons-protectia-consumatorului-de-patrimoniu-marea-moschee-sau-moscheea-carol-i-din-constanta/)
  for the order of Carol I in 1910, the 1822 mosque it replaces, Victor
  Ștefănescu, the Konya model, reinforced concrete (Wikipedia says the first in
  Romania, InfoCons one of the first: the card says "fra le prime") and the
  inauguration on 31 May 1913. Minaret height (45 or 47 m) is omitted. The 140
  steps of the minaret come from several travel pages.
- Casino: [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/constanta/fascinanta-poveste-a-cazinoului-din-constanta-1607801.html)
  and [Radio Romania International](https://www.rri.ro/en/features-and-reports/rri-encyclopaedia/the-casino-in-constanta-id169639.html)
  for Daniel Renard's Art Nouveau design, replaced by Petre Antonescu's and
  restored as the local administration changed, the inauguration on 15 August
  1910 (Adevărul and Playtech), the post-1948 house of culture and the restaurant.
  [G4Media](https://www.g4media.ro/cazinoul-din-constanta-redeschis-10-000-de-vizitatori-in-patru-zile.html)
  and [Gândul](https://www.gandul.ro/actualitate/cazinoul-constanta-cladirea-simbol-a-litoralului-romanesc-redeschisa-dupa-5-ani-de-lucrari-cand-poate-fi-vizitat-monumentul-20524365)
  for the reopening in May 2025 after five years of works.

No opening hours or ticket prices are introduced: the mosaic and the casino send
the reader to the official sites.

## Verified (2026-11-13)

- The text gives no figures for 1896, only the groups living in the city around
  1900; the Jewish community (957 people, 6.5% in 1899) is confirmed by the
  [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://encyclopedia.com/religion/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/constanta).
- The minaret can be climbed (about 140 steps): Lonely Planet,
  [Euronews România](https://www.euronews.ro/articole/romania-frumoasa-din-constanta-octogon-confesional-centrul-istoric-suberek-piperc).
- The casino (Bulevardul Regina Elisabeta 4) and the Genoese lighthouse (Strada
  Remus Opreanu 17) are about 200 m apart on the promenade (OpenStreetMap).
- Stock photographs: done (Wikimedia Commons, with author and licence in `structure.ts`).

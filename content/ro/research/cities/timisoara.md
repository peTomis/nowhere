# Timișoara city card

Timișoara is in the Banat, which the trip filter has no entry for. As agreed with
the author, the city is filed under Transilvania (as the guide already does for the
Decebalus rock sculpture under Valacchia); the introduction says it is the Banat,
with a history distinct from the principality of Transylvania, and the traces'
place line reads "Timișoara, Banato". Three places are appended to the album. The
1989 event of the timeline links to two of them with `<Today>`, gated on
Timișoara.

## Evidence consulted

- City history: [History of Timișoara](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/History_of_Timisoara),
  [Siege of Temeşvar (1716)](<https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Siege_of_Teme%C5%9Fvar_(1716)>),
  [Explore Carpathia](https://www.explorecarpathia.eu/en/romania/temesvar-timisoara)
  and the [Analele Banatului article by Costin Feneșan](https://analelebanatului.ro/en/view-article/399/)
  for the Ottoman conquest in 1552, about 160 years of Ottoman rule, Eugene of
  Savoy's conquest in 1716, the Banat of Temeswar as a separate Habsburg province,
  the rebuilding by Austrian engineers and the passage to Romania in 1919. The
  surrender day (12 or 13 October 1716) is omitted. German-speaking settlers and
  the 2023 European Capital of Culture title are general knowledge not corroborated
  by the pages opened: check them before publication.
- Piața Unirii: [Heritage of Timișoara](https://heritageoftimisoara.ro/en/buildings/Cetate/address/Unirii/12),
  [Zile și Nopți](https://zilesinopti.ro/?p=599076) and
  [Union Square, Timișoara](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Union_Square,_Timi%C8%99oara)
  for the Roman Catholic cathedral of St George (1736–1774), the oldest square of
  the city and the renaming after 1919. The Serbian Orthodox cathedral and the Holy
  Trinity column are named without dates or authors (a single source attributes
  the column to Georg Raphael Donner, 1739–1740).
- Tőkés parsonage: [László Tőkés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A1szl%C3%B3_T%C5%91k%C3%A9s),
  [Rough Guides](https://m.roughguides.com/romania/the-banat-and-crisana/timisoara/)
  and [eMaramureș](https://www.emaramures.ro/15-decembrie-1989-timisoara-aici-a-inceput-drumul-spre-libertate)
  for the eviction order, the gathering of 15 December in Piața Maria and the
  protest widening the next day.
- Opera balcony: [Europa Liberă](https://romania.europalibera.org/a/decembrie-1989-timisoara/32734358.html),
  [Digi24](https://www.digi24.ro/stiri/actualitate/evenimente/24-de-ani-de-la-revolutie-pe-20-decembrie-1989-timisoara-a-devenit-primul-oras-liber-de-comunism-172238)
  and [G4Media](https://www.g4media.ro/20-decembrie-1989-ziua-in-care-timisoara-se-declara-oras-liber-de-comunism-sirenele-au-rasunat-din-nou-in-oras-pentru-a-aminti-ce-s-a-intamplat-in-urma-cu-35-de-ani.html)
  for 20 December, the proclamation from the Opera balcony and the sirens at noon.
  Crowd sizes (50,000 to 150,000) and death tolls (66 to over 90) differ between
  sources: the card says "una folla enorme" and "decine di morti".

No opening hours, ticket prices or interior access are introduced.

## Verified (2026-11-13)

- 2023 European Capital of Culture, with Elefsina and Veszprém
  ([Europa Liberă](https://romania.europalibera.org/amp/timisoara-capitala-europeana-a-culturii/32275512.html));
  German-speaking settlers in the Banat from the early 18th century, again
  under Maria Theresa and Joseph II
  ([Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-interne/societate/destinul-dramatic-al-colonistilor-svabi-din-banat-2523404.html)).
- The parish house is in the same building as the Reformed church (1902),
  Strada Timotei Cipariu 1 on Piața Maria
  ([Heritage of Timișoara](https://heritageoftimisoara.ro/en/buildings/Elisabetin/address/Timotei+Cipariu/1)).
  "Accanto alla chiesa" is close enough; "nello stesso edificio" would be exact.
- Stock photographs: done (Wikimedia Commons, with author and licence in `structure.ts`).

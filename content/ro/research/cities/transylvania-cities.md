# Brașov, Sibiu and Sighișoara city cards

These cards extend the Cities chapter with a Transylvania part. Each has three
traces. Eight places are appended to the album; Sighișoara reuses the existing Vlad
Dracul house, with a city-specific description. Existing IDs, numbers and photos
are preserved. The introductions interpret urban form rather than repeat the
country timeline.

## Evidence consulted

- Brașov: the [county library's local heritage overview](https://www.bjbv.ro/en/brasov-and-its-surroundings/local-heritage/)
  supports the commercial square, surviving Catherine Gate and relationship with
  Șchei. The [history museum's Council House record](https://www.brasovistorie.ro/despre-muzeu/istoria-cladirilor/casa-sfatului)
  documents municipal rooms above the furriers' commercial premises.
  The [Black Church parish history](https://bisericaneagra.ro/en/the-history-of-the-black-church/)
  supplies the church's history and the 1689 fire. No superlatives about size or
  carpet collections are reproduced. Catherine Gate is distinguished from the
  later Șchei Gate; its precise construction date is not needed in the card.
- Sibiu: the [Brukenthal Museum history](https://www.brukenthalmuseum.ro/mnb/istoric_detaliu_en/1088)
  documents the collection and public opening in 1817; the
  [municipal gallery page](https://sibiucity.ro/ro/muzeu/galeriile-de-arta.-palatul-brukenthal)
  describes the palace and European art collection.
  The [municipal bridge record](https://www.sibiucity.ro/en/detalii/180) and
  [local travel guide](https://sibiutravelguide.com/attractions/bridge-of-lies/)
  support its position and the attributed legend. The text uses the neutral
  term “metal bridge”, avoiding inconsistent technical descriptions.
  The [municipal cathedral record](https://patrimoniu.sibiu.ro/biserici_en/evanghelica/53),
  [city visitor app](https://www.sibiucityapp.ro/catedrala-evanghelica?locale=en)
  and [Lutheran parish](https://hermannstadt.evang.ro/ro/) support the Gothic
  building at Piața Huet and its religious community.
- Sighișoara: [UNESCO's property record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/902/) and
  [ICOMOS evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/154557) underpin the fortified
  merchant town, upper/lower settlement and rebuilding after fire.
  The local visitor guide's [Clock Tower](https://ghid.exploresighisoara.ro/en/obiectiv/turnul-cu-ceas)
  and [covered stairway](https://ghid.exploresighisoara.ro/en/obiectiv/scara-acoperita)
  entries supply orientation details. Their colourful legends are not repeated.
  Step counts and a construction date for the stairway are omitted. The existing
  distinction between the Vlad birthplace tradition and documented fact is retained.

The sources above were opened during research. A Catherine Gate heritage database
entry timed out, its tourism-app URL redirected to the homepage, and the RAN record
failed to load; those pages are not counted as corroboration. The gate description
therefore stays within the county library's evidence. This record is not external
historical review. No opening hours, ticket prices or guaranteed interior access
are introduced.

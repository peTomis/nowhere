# Natura: Wadi al-Hitan e Wadi el-Rayan (Basso Egitto)

Landscape card `eg.landscape.wadi-al-hitan`, traces `wadi-al-hitan` (remote, rare) and
`wadi-el-rayan` (remote, rare). Pages read in full on 2026-11-25. UNESCO's pages
(whc.unesco.org/en/list/1186) answer 403 to every automated read: the facts UNESCO would give are
taken from the pages below.

## Facts and their sources

- A shallow sea (Tethys) about 40 million years ago: NatGeo ("40 million years ago… Tethys"),
  IUGS ("spanning 40 million years"); Wikipedia gives 40–41 Ma for the Gehannam Formation. Shark
  teeth and shells in the rock: NatGeo, Wikipedia.
- Rocks sculpted by the wind: NatGeo, Wikipedia (and the Commons photo _Wind erosion in Wadi
  Al-Hitan_).
- First skeletons "at the beginning of the 20th century": Wikipedia gives winter 1902–03, IUGS
  names Beadnell (1905). The two do not agree on the year, so the text gives none.
- UNESCO World Heritage 2005: Wikipedia, IUGS.
- Within the Wadi El-Rayan Protected Area: Wikipedia, IUGS (Special Protected Area 1997, not used).
- About a dozen skeletons along a path: NatGeo ("about a dozen"); Wikipedia ("a number of partial
  skeletons… walkways between the main fossils"). Basilosaurus and Dorudon: NatGeo, Wikipedia.
  Largest skeleton 21 m: Wikipedia, IUGS. Hind legs with feet and toes: NatGeo, Wikipedia, IUGS.
- Access: 4WD on an unpaved track (Wikipedia, NatGeo "long unsealed road"); guided along a
  prescribed trail (Wikipedia); about 1.5 h from Faiyum (NatGeo). Winter: Wikipedia (visitors camp
  on winter weekends).
- Wadi el-Rayan: drainage water from Faiyum since 1973 (PMC, Lake El Rayan); Wondermondo says
  works began in 1974. The text says "dagli anni Settanta". Two lakes at different heights, below
  sea level (PMC: upper lake −10 m; Wondermondo: −14 m and −36 m). The falls formed where the
  stream between the lakes eroded its bed (Wondermondo; Wikipedia "waterfalls between the two").
  A popular local outing: Wondermondo, Lake El Rayan (Tourism).

# Natura: La costa del Mar Rosso (Mar Rosso)

Landscape card `eg.landscape.red-sea-coast`, traces `giftun` (nearby, common), `abu-dabbab`
(nearby, common), `samadai` (nearby, rare), `wadi-el-gemal` (remote, rare). Pages read on
2026-11-25. No FCDO advice for the Red Sea coast.

## Pages

- Key Biodiversity Areas, factsheet 6201, Hurghada Archipelago (KBA).
- HEPCA, "Samadai project" and "Abu Dabab Conservation Initiative".
- Journal of the Marine Biological Association of the UK, "Spinner dolphin… resting habitat in
  Samadai Reef… protected through tourism management" (JMBA, abstract).
- Mongabay, "'A very successful story': An Egypt tribe welcomes tourists & protects its coast"
  (2025).
- Wikipedia: Giftun Islands, Marsa Alam, Wadi El Gemal National Park (as check).

## Facts and their sources

- Islands of fossil coral raised around a core of igneous rock: KBA (for the archipelago; the
  text says it of the islands in general, not of Giftun).
- Seagrass, green turtles, dugongs: HEPCA (Abu Dabab), Wikipedia (Wadi El Gemal).
- Dolphins resting in reef lagoons by day: HEPCA (Samadai), JMBA.
- Ports, quarries, mines and the Ababda: the country sheet §2; Ababda: Mongabay.
- Hurghada from a town with two hotels to a resort city in the 1990s: KBA ("during the past
  decade", written about 2001). Marsa Alam and its airport: Wikipedia (airport 2001/2003; the
  text gives no year).
- Protection: the islands south of 27°15'N since 1986 (KBA; Giftun is at 27°12'), Wadi el-Gemal
  2003 (Wikipedia, search results "January 2003").
- Giftun: two islands (Wikipedia), uninhabited (KBA), white beaches and boat trips from Hurghada
  (Wikipedia; Mahmya and Orange Bay are OpenStreetMap beaches on the island). No overnight stays:
  travel pages in the search (**one kind of source**).
- Abu Dabbab: one of the largest seagrass patches, dugongs, turtle nesting (HEPCA); turtles
  (Wikipedia, Marsa Alam). Access only from the shore, no boats in the bay, buoyed zones, rangers
  (HEPCA; the page is undated). About 30 km north of Marsa Alam: Wikidata coordinates.
- Samadai: a lagoon sheltered from the north winds where spinner dolphins rest by day (HEPCA,
  JMBA: entering at daybreak, leaving in the afternoon); up to 30 boats and 500–800 people a day in
  the early 2000s (HEPCA); management from 2004, zones, the dolphins' zone closed (JMBA, HEPCA, the
  2009 Star-Bulletin report in the search: "75 percent closed", population stable). Limited
  visitors and a fee: HEPCA (200 a day, in the search) and the search results.
- Wadi el-Gemal: national park 2003 (Wikipedia, search); gazelles and ibex (Wikipedia, both
  pages); the Ababda and al-Qula'an with its mangroves (Mongabay); visits by 4x4 with a guide
  (search results, German travel page).

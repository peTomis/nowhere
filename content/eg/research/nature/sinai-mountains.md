# Natura: Le montagne del Sinai (Sinai)

Landscape card `eg.landscape.sinai-mountains`, traces `st-catherines-monastery` and `mount-sinai`
(both remote, rare). Pages read on 2026-11-25. UNESCO's page (whc.unesco.org/en/list/954) answers
403 to automated reads. St Catherine is on the St Catherine–Nuweiba road, the boundary of FCDO's
advice; the route from Sharm el-Sheikh and Dahab is outside the advised area.

## Facts and their sources

- Mount Catherine the highest peak of Egypt: Wikipedia, Rough Guides. Its height differs
  (2,629 m / over 2,640 m): not in the text.
- Christian hermits from the 3rd century, the pilgrim Egeria in the 380s: Sacred Land, Wikipedia
  ("fin dai primi secoli cristiani").
- Monastery built by Justinian, 548–565: Egymonuments, Wikipedia; Rough Guides "6th century".
- World Heritage 2002: Egymonuments, Wikipedia.
- Jabaliya Bedouin, gardens in the valleys, guides: Sacred Land; Wikipedia (guides mandatory).
- Roads and an airport during the Israeli occupation 1967–1982: Sacred Land; the occupation dates
  also on Wikipedia (Blue Hole page).
- Camels for centuries: Sacred Land (pilgrims "by foot and camel"), Wikipedia (Loti's journey by
  camel, 1895). Phrased as "a dorso di cammello".
- Monastery: Fatimid mosque inside (Egymonuments: 12th c.; Wikipedia: 1106), library and
  manuscripts (both), burning bush (both, and Sacred Land), "one of the oldest continuously
  inhabited" (Wikipedia, Rough Guides, Egymonuments "one of the oldest functioning"), Greek
  Orthodox (Wikipedia, Sacred Land).
- Mount Sinai 2,285 m: Wikipedia, Sacred Destinations, Rough Guides. Chapel of 1934 and a mosque
  at the summit: Wikipedia, Sacred Destinations, Sacred Land. Two routes, the camel path and the
  3,750 steps: Wikipedia, Rough Guides (Lonely Planet in the search). About 2.5 hours: Wikipedia,
  Rough Guides. Night climb for sunrise: Wikipedia, Rough Guides. Guide mandatory: Wikipedia and
  travel pages in the search. Blankets and tea along the path: Wikipedia.

## Visiting the monastery (changes over time)

Open mornings, closed Fridays, Sundays and Orthodox holidays: several 2026 travel guides (hours
differ: 8:45–11:45, 9–12). In May 2025 the Ismailia Court of Appeal ruled that the monastery's
lands are state property with usufruct for the monks (Wikipedia); the gates were briefly closed,
visits resumed (Travel2Egypt, early 2026). A Court of Cassation ruling was announced for 24
October 2026 (ad-hoc-news); **no report of the outcome found on 2026-11-25**: `TODO verificare`
in `it/names.ts`. The «Come trovarla» gives no hours and says to check them.

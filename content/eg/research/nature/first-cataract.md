# Natura: La prima cateratta (Alto Egitto)

Landscape card `eg.landscape.first-cataract`, traces `felucca-cataract` and `botanical-island`
(both city, Aswan, common). Pages read on 2026-11-25.

## Pages

- Saudi Aramco World, "Gateway on the Nile" (1985) (Aramco).
- Frommer's, "Kitchener Island".
- Wikipedia: Cataracts of the Nile, El Nabatat Island (as check).

## Facts and their sources

- Rapids among rocks and islets just south of Aswan: Wikipedia ("a stretch… just south of
  Aswan… granite outcroppings"), Aramco ("torrents of white water foaming over… outcroppings of
  rock").
- The cataract as Egypt's southern border for most of its history: Wikipedia, Aramco ("an
  impassable barrier", "the end of the known world").
- Granite quarried at Aswan and floated downstream to Thebes and Memphis; pink, red, grey: Aramco
  ("pink, red, blue and black… floating the stone downstream to Thebes, Abydos, Memphis"); the
  quarries also in the Rough Guides page used by the search (not cited).
- The first Aswan dam, 1902, submerged the rapids: Wikipedia, Aramco (built 1902, raised 1912 and
  1933).
- Granite islands polished by the floods: Aramco. Feluccas around the islands to Kitchener's
  island: Aramco, Wikipedia (El Nabatat).
- Botanical Island: under 1 km (Wikipedia: 750 m; the text says "meno di un chilometro"); owned
  by Kitchener and made a garden of exotic plants (Wikipedia, Frommer's; where they came from,
  India for Wikipedia, also Africa for travel pages, is left out); later a state research station (Wikipedia,
  search snippets); palm-lined walkways (Frommer's). Boats land on the south-eastern side
  (Wikipedia).

## Left out

- Kitchener's dates and role: Wikipedia ties the gift of the island to his years as consul
  general (1911–1914), Frommer's and Lonely Planet to the 1890s, when he commanded the Egyptian
  army. The text gives neither.
- Entry fees.

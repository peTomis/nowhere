# Natura: stock images

The stock image of every nature trace (B9: shown in greyscale until the reader adds a photo).
Chosen on 2026-11-25 on Wikimedia Commons, searched by place name; each licence and author read
from the file's metadata. Openverse (for Flickr) refused automated requests. Every file is
cropped to 960x720 and saved as WebP under `public/guides/egypt/traces/<slug>.webp`.

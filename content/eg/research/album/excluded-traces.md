## Fesikh (was 64, Basso Egitto, food)

Taken out on 2026-11-24, while writing the Gastronomia chapter, for the country sheet's safety
rule (§13: the guide must never create problems for whoever travels; only safe gestures).

Fesikh is grey mullet salted and fermented for weeks, the dish of Sham el-Nessim, the spring
holiday on the Monday after Coptic Easter. Egypt's Ministry of Health warns against it every
year because of botulism ([Egypt Today, 2020](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/84888/Egypt’s-traditional-fesikh-dish-can-cause-botulism-poisoning-Health-Ministry),
[Egypt Today, 2022](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/115315/Health-Ministry-tightens-control-on-Fesikh-factories-reiterates-warning-against));
the 1991 outbreak after Sham el-Nessim killed 18 people
([Bibliotheca Alexandrina](https://www.bibalex.org/sciplanet/en/article/details?id=12393)). A
trace with the gesture "taste" would ask the reader to do what the national health authority
advises against; a trace of the fish on a shop counter would be a filler.

It can come back as text: a Context on Sham el-Nessim in the timeline (with the coloured eggs,
green onions and herring) states the custom and the yearly warning as facts.

Draft texts: name «Il fesikh»; place «Delta, Basso Egitto»; find «Nelle botteghe di pesce salato
nei giorni prima di Sham el-Nessim».

## Tè beduino (was 128, Sinai, food)

Taken out on 2026-11-25 (user's review of the food traces) as a duplicate (B13, "Doppioni"):
the same drink as the shai of the ahwa, and the same photo, a glass of tea. Three food traces
were a glass of an infusion (shai, Bedouin tea, karkadè). The Bedouin way of making it stays as
text: the Context «Il tè sul fuoco» in the tea card, with its sources (The National, National
Geographic, Atmos) now under the common food part. The Sinai part of the Gastronomia chapter is
gone with it; Sinai has 9 traces (a small region, declared).

Draft texts: name «Il tè dei beduini»; place «Sinai meridionale, Sinai»; find «Offerto nei campi
beduini e lungo i sentieri intorno a Santa Caterina, fatto bollire sul fuoco.» Stock image: Hesham
Farouk Ragab, _شاي بدوي.jpg_, CC0 (St Catherine, 2022); still in the bucket, out of the manifest.

## Le dune del Grande Mare di Sabbia (was 109, Deserto occidentale, nature)

Taken out on 2026-11-25, while writing the Natura chapter, for the country sheet's safety rule
(§13). The UK FCDO advice (read 2026-11-25) excepts from its warning on the Western Desert only
"the oasis town of Siwa" and the Marsa Matruh–Siwa road, and advises against the Bahariya–Siwa
road; the dunes of the Great Sand Sea are reached by 4x4 trips out of the oasis (Bir Wahed and
beyond), outside the exception. A trace would send the reader where the advice says not to go.
The Sand Sea stays as a line of the Siwa landscape card, now «Le acque di Siwa»
(`../nature/siwa-waters.md`), whose second trace is Cleopatra's spring.

Draft texts: name «Le dune del Grande Mare di Sabbia»; place «Siwa, Deserto occidentale»; find
«In fuoristrada da Siwa, con un operatore autorizzato e il permesso del ministero dell’Interno».

# Egypt — list of places (album draft)

Step A11 / B13 of `docs/prompt-for-new-guide.md`, built on the confirmed country sheet
(`../country-sheet.md`). **A proposal, not verified**: every row still has to pass the six tests
with two sources, coordinates from Wikidata/OpenStreetMap, a licensed stock image and the safety
check of the sheet's §13. Titles are the Italian ones the album would show; notes are English.

Numbers start at 04 (01–03 are cover, flag, coat of arms) and follow the album's pages: whole
country first, then region by region. They can still change freely: nothing is published.

Columns: **where** `city` / `nearby` / `remote` / `region` / `anywhere`; **value** `C` common
(bronze, 1 pt), `R` rare (silver, 2), `VR` very rare (gold, 3); **theme** `A` antiquity
(prehistory to Roman, monuments and objects), `H` Coptic, Islamic and modern heritage, `C` living
culture, pop and Egypt today, `N` nature, `F` food and drink; **gesture** what the reader does
besides looking. **Card**: person, city, landscape (`L:`), food (`F:`) or the timeline's "Oggi"
(`Today:` + the event).

## Safety check (sheet §13)

**Reference used: UK FCDO travel advice for Egypt**, updated 27 October 2026, read 2026-11-24
(https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/egypt/warnings-and-insurance and
https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/egypt/safety-and-security). The Farnesina's _Viaggiare

FCDO advises against **all travel** to North Sinai and within 20 km of the Libyan border (El Salloum
excepted), and against **all but essential travel** to: inland South Sinai north of the St
Catherine–Nuweiba road (coasts excepted), Ismailia governorate east of the Canal, Hala'ib and Bir
Tawil, and the Western Desert — with exceptions for Luxor, Qena, Aswan, Abu Simbel, Fayyum, the
coast from the Delta to Marsa Matruh, the Marsa Matruh–Siwa road and Siwa town, the White and Black
Deserts, the oasis towns of Bahariya, Farafra, Dakhla and Kharga and the roads between them (the
Giza–Farafra road with 50 km either side). The Bahariya–Siwa road is not excepted.

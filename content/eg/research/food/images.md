# Gastronomia: stock images

The stock image of every food trace (B9: shown in greyscale until the reader adds a photo).
Chosen 2026-11-25 on Wikimedia Commons (searched by name in English and Arabic and through the
Egypt categories: _Cuisine of Egypt_, _Falafel of Egypt_, _Tea of Egypt_, _Tea drinking in
Egypt_, _Sugar cane juice industry in Egypt_, _Dates in Egypt_, _Bedouins of Sinai_, _Ababda in
Egypt_) and through Openverse for Flickr. Each licence was read on the file's page. Every file is
cropped to 960x720 and saved as WebP under `public/guides/egypt/traces/`, then pushed with
`yarn assets push`.

Notes:

- **Siwa dates**: no freely licensed photograph of dates _in Siwa_ was found; every photo in
  _Dates in Egypt_ is from Giza (Dahshur, Badrashin) or Cairo. The image is Siwa's palm groves,
  where the dates come from, rather than dates from elsewhere.
- **Jabana**: the only photo found of the gabbana in Egypt. It is a portrait of two Ababda men,
  with the pot on the embers in the foreground; the crop keeps both. Sudanese and Ethiopian
  jebena photos on Commons were left out (B6: not another country's object for an Egyptian
  trace). Flickr gives CC BY 2.0 (checked on the photo's page; Openverse said BY-SA).
- **Eish shamsi**: the only Commons file (العيش الشمسي.png) is a frame from a YouTube video by
  an unnamed author, uploaded as CC BY 3.0 by someone else: the licence cannot be verified, so it
  is not used. Roland Unger's _EgFoodAswanBakery.jpg_ shows a bakery in Aswan, not this bread.
- **Om ali**: the Khan el-Khalili photo (Fred Cherrygarden) is Egyptian, but its burnt top hides
  the nuts and raisins that the card describes.

- **Bedouin tea**: trace removed 2026-11-25 (`../album/excluded-traces.md`); its image was
  Hesham Farouk Ragab, _شاي بدوي.jpg_, CC0.

## Cover and emblems

Not food, kept here with the other images until the Identità chapter has its own notes.

- **Cover** (trace 01): _All Gizah Pyramids.jpg_, Ricardo Liberato, CC BY-SA 2.0: the pyramids
  of Giza from the desert to the south-west, sky at the top for the logo, sand at the bottom for
  the name. 3840 px JPEG. `coverInk` is the text color, as for Romania: dark on the sky and on
  the light gradient at the bottom.
- **Coat of arms** (03): _Coat of arms of Egypt (Official).svg_, public domain, the gold and
  white version of the Eagle of Saladin, the same as on the flag (the other Commons file is gold
  and black). Rendered to a 480 px PNG.
- **Flag** (02): _Flag of Egypt.svg_, public domain, used as it is.

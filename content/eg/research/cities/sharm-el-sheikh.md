# Città: Sharm el-Sheikh (Sinai)

City card `eg.sharm-el-sheikh`, two traces. Pages read on 2026-11-25: Egyptian Streets and
Al-Monitor on the al-Sahaba mosque; Rough Guides "Sharm El Sheikh"; Wikipedia (Sharm El Sheikh).

- Fishing town and military base; developed under the Israeli occupation 1967–1982 (airport,
  Ofira, the first hotels at Naama Bay) and then by Egypt; conferences, "City of Peace":
  Wikipedia; Britannica and NPR in the search.
- **Old market and al-Sahaba mosque**: opened 24 March 2017, by Fouad Tawfik Hafez, Mamluk and
  Ottoman styles, at the centre of the old market: Al-Monitor, Egyptian Streets. No image: a
  building of 2017.
- **Naama Bay**: where the first hotel was opened during the occupation (NPR, Britannica in the
  search; one hotel's self-description), the promenade and beaches: Rough Guides.

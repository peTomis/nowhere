# Città: Alessandria (Basso Egitto)

City card `eg.alexandria`, seven traces. Pages read on 2026-11-25: the Bibliotheca Alexandrina's
"Places to see" (Bibalex); the ministry's Qaitbay Fort page; Stanford Humanities Center and The
Irish Times on the Trianon; Scene Now on the Cavafy Museum; Wikipedia as check (Alexandria,
Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa, Pompey's Pillar, Citadel of Qaitbay, Abu al-Abbas al-Mursi Mosque,
Mario Rossi, Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Cavafy Museum, Constantine P. Cavafy).

## Introduction

- Founded by Alexander in 331 BC; Ptolemaic capital; 641 conquest; decline; the Mahmoudiyah canal
  of 1820; Europeans leaving after 1956 and the nationalisations: Wikipedia (Alexandria); Rough
  Guides (founded by Alexander); Majalla and Stanford (the cosmopolitan city and its cafés, in the
  search). Greek, Italian and Jewish communities: Wikipedia and the Stanford piece.

## Traces

- **Kom el-Shoqafa**: 2nd century AD, found in 1900 when a donkey fell in, Egyptian and
  Greco-Roman fusion: Bibalex, Wikipedia; Anubis in Roman armour: Wikipedia and the Commons photo.
- **Pompey's Pillar**: red Aswan granite, almost 27 m, the only ancient monument standing, in the
  Serapeum: Bibalex; for Diocletian (298–302), the misread name: Wikipedia.
- **Qaitbay fort**: 1477–1479 on the site of the Pharos, destroyed by earthquakes in 1303 and 1323:
  ministry, Wikipedia, Bibalex.
- **Abu al-Abbas al-Mursi**: Sufi from Murcia, 13th century, pilgrimage: Bibalex. Rebuilt with
  Mario Rossi from the 1930s (construction 16–18 years; 1929–45 in one list): Wikipedia (both
  pages), the search results.
- **Bibliotheca Alexandrina**: inaugurated 2002, the tilted disc, granite wall with 120 scripts,
  planetarium: Wikipedia; the library's own site for the rest. **Only Wikipedia** for the 120
  scripts: to recheck on bibalex.org.
- **Cavafy Museum**: Cavafy's apartment until his death in 1933 (Wikipedia); reopened in May 2024
  after restoration, run by Greek foundations (Scene Now, Onassis Foundation via search). Hours:
  one travel page only (Tuesday–Sunday): the text says to check.
- **Trianon**: open, Saad Zaghloul square, art nouveau, Cavafy's office upstairs: Irish Times,
  Stanford, Fodor's (address, via search). Founding year disputed (1905 "allegedly"): the text
  says "all'inizio del Novecento".

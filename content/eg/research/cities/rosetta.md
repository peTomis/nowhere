# Città: Rosetta (Basso Egitto)

City card `eg.rosetta`, three traces. Pages read on 2026-11-25: Rough Guides "Rosetta (Rashid)";
Wikipedia (Rosetta, Fort Julien). Travel2Egypt's article failed to load; its content and Real
Egypt's come through the search.

- Port on the western branch, 65 km from Alexandria, booming after 1517 and declining after
  Alexandria's revival; the Rosetta Stone found by French soldiers in 1799, now in the British
  Museum: Wikipedia, Rough Guides.
- Houses of the 18th–19th centuries, brickwork with joints painted white or red, mashrabiya;
  Amasyali house's ceiling and mother-of-pearl: Rough Guides; the houses also in Real Egypt and
  Travel2Egypt (search).
- **Open or closed**: Rough Guides gives the Amasyali house and the Abu Shahin mill open with one
  ticket; Travel2Egypt says the house is closed and the mill open; Real Egypt lists the house open.
  The «Come trovarla» says the house has sometimes been closed and to ask at the mill.
- Mill around 1808, for a Turkish agha who left both to his servant Amasyali: Rough Guides.
  **One page**: the Commons photo is "Tahona House", the mill (tahuna).
- Fort: built about 1470 by Qaitbay with reused ancient stones, rebuilt by the French as Fort
  Julien in 1799, the Stone found there; restored and reopened in 1985, open to the public:
  Wikipedia, tour pages in the search (Connolly Cove). **Opening hours unverified.**

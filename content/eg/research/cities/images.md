# Città: stock images

Chosen on 2026-11-25 on Wikimedia Commons; licence and author read from each file's metadata.
Cropped to 960x720 WebP under `public/guides/egypt/traces/<slug>.webp`.

Egypt has no freedom of panorama for works still under copyright (life plus 50 years): recent
buildings get a `TODO image` instead of a photo. That is the case of the Cave Church (1970s and
later), the Bibliotheca Alexandrina (2002), Hassan Fathy's New Gourna (Fathy died in 1989) and the
al-Sahaba mosque (2017). Mario Rossi died in 1961 and Alexandre Marcel in 1928: their buildings
are out of copyright.

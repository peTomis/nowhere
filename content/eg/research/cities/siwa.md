# Città: Siwa (Deserto occidentale)

City card `eg.siwa`, three traces. Pages read on 2026-11-25: Rough Guides "Siwa Oasis"; NASA
Earth Observatory; Memphis Tours "Gebel al Mawta Siwa"; Wikipedia (Siwa Oasis). Lonely Planet,
Fodor's and Egypt Today refused automated reads.

- Isolated until the 1980s road to Marsa Matruh; Berber people and the Siwi language; annexed by
  Muhammad Ali in 1820; Shali abandoned after the rains of 1926: Wikipedia, Rough Guides.
- **Shali**: kershef (salt and mud brick), the climb and the view, approached from the south-west,
  lit at night: Wikipedia, Rough Guides.
- **Oracle temple**: in the ruins of Aghurmi; Alexander hailed as son of Amun: Wikipedia, NASA.
  The year of Alexander's visit is not given.
- **Gebel al-Mawta**: rock-cut tombs from the Late Period to Roman times, just north of town:
  Wikipedia, Memphis Tours. Si-Amun's tomb, a Greek landowner praying to Egyptian gods: Memphis
  Tours; the search results (Lonely Planet: beard and curly hair) could not be read: left out.
  Used as a shelter in the Second World War: Memphis Tours only, left out.

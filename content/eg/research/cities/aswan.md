# Città: Assuan (Alto Egitto e Nubia)

City card `eg.aswan`, `on` Aswan and Nubia (Philae and Gharb Soheil are in Nubia, country sheet
§2). Pages read on 2026-11-25: Rough Guides "Aswan"; Saudi Aramco World "Gateway on the Nile";
the ministry's Unfinished Obelisk page; Egypt Tours Plus on Daraw; UN Tourism "Gharb Suhayl";
Mandarin Oriental's Agatha Christie Suite page; Wikipedia as check (Aswan, Qubbet el-Hawa, Old
Cataract Hotel, Philae, Graffito of Esmet-Akhom).

- Elephantine as the border town, granite quarries, the dams and Lake Nasser: Rough Guides,
  Aramco, Wikipedia.
- **Unfinished obelisk**: 42 m, abandoned for a crack, perhaps under Hatshepsut: Rough Guides,
  ministry, Wikipedia.
- **Qubbet el-Hawa**: tombs of Elephantine's governors, visible from the Corniche, steep climb:
  Rough Guides, Wikipedia. Harkhuf as an expedition leader: Wikipedia. Harkhuf's letter about the
  dancing dwarf (planned): not in the pages read, left out.
- **Old Cataract**: 1899 by Thomas Cook (opened January 1900): Rough Guides, Wikipedia. Agatha
  Christie and "Death on the Nile", the 1978 film: Rough Guides, the hotel's suite page.
- **Daraw**: camels from Sudan along the Forty Days Road, about 40 km north of Aswan: travel pages
  in the search. Market days disagree (Tuesday; Saturday and Sunday): the text says to ask.
  Photos of children are prohibited (FCDO, country sheet §13).
- **Philae**: the last hieroglyphic inscription, 24 August 394: Wikipedia, Greek Reporter (search);
  moved to Agilkia 1977–1980 by two Italian companies: Wikipedia, Aramco (the move).
- **Gharb Soheil**: domed mud-brick houses painted with birds, palms, flowers, many now
  guesthouses: UN Tourism, The National and travel pages (search). Nubian lands flooded by Lake
  Nasser in the 1960s: the same search, Aramco.

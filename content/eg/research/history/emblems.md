## Flag

- Three equal horizontal bands, red, white, black; the eagle of Saladin in gold in the white band:
  Constitution art. 223 ("three colors black, white, and red with the eagle of Saladin in golden
  yellow"), WorldAtlas, FOTW. Desecration a crime: Constitution, FOTW (SIS: penal provisions).
- Colours from the flag of the 1952 revolution, which ended the monarchy: WorldAtlas, FOTW
  (SIS; Fruhlinger's note). The July 23, 1952 revolution: also the Constitution's preamble.
- Official symbolism (red the struggle against the British before 1952, white the bloodless
  revolution, black the end of the oppression of monarchy and colonialism): SIS via FOTW,
  WorldAtlas (same three readings). The text calls it "la spiegazione ufficiale": FOTW also gives
  other readings (Whitney Smith: red revolution, white future, black past; the "black land" of
  ancient Egypt, called pseudo-history there).
- Kingdom: green with a white crescent and three stars: SIS via FOTW (1923), WorldAtlas (1922).
- 1958, United Arab Republic, two green stars: SIS via FOTW, WorldAtlas. 1972, a golden hawk:
  SIS via FOTW, WorldAtlas (flag of 1972–1984). 4 October 1984, the eagle: FOTW (adoption date,
  SIS "in 1984"), WorldAtlas (header; its text also says 1982, in conflict with every other page,
  not used).
- Left out: the King's name (Farouk: SIS only, WorldAtlas wrongly says Fuad I); the 1952–1958
  flag in detail (SIS omits it); the shared colours of other Arab flags (B6, and FOTW only).

## Coat of arms

- The golden eagle with a shield in the flag's colours on its breast and a scroll with
  "Jumhuriyat Misr al-Arabiya" ("Arab Republic of Egypt"): WorldAtlas, FOTW (inscription; the
  shield's three parts in the colour variants). Kufic script: Wikipedia only, left out.
- Saladin, the 12th-century sultan of Egypt and Syria: SIS via FOTW; he built the Citadel of
  Cairo (work begun 1176): Aramco World.
- A modern state symbol, from the 1952 revolution: FOTW (Smith: "the use of the Saladin eagle on
  Arab flags is modern"), WorldAtlas (the 1952 tricolour with the eagle). Adopted again in 1984:
  FOTW ("flag and coat of arms adopted 4 October 1984"), WorldAtlas.
- Left out, **to verify**: the stone eagle on the west wall of the Citadel, the supposed model of
  the emblem, now headless, probably moved there under Muhammad Ali (Wikipedia citing Nasser
  Rabbat, _The Citadel of Cairo_, 1995, p. 24; FOTW citing Whitney Smith: "based on the eagle
  carved on a wall in Cairo"). Rabbat's book not read. Worth a trace at the Citadel if a second
  source confirms where it is.
- Left out: the name order Jumhuriya al-Arabiya al-Misriya → Jumhuriyat Misr al-Arabiya ("Egypt
  first, then Arab": FOTW only).
